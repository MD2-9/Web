date_range 11. Date & Time Pickers

The Date Picker and Time Picker, built with 0px sharp corners and 50% circular geometries, are anchored inside the Right Component Panel:

  • Desktop (≥768px): Permanently docked on the right with a draggable edge resizer (Double-click toggles 290px / 629px);
  • Mobile (<768px): Hidden off-screen, summoned via bottom-left menu or right-to-left swipe gesture;
  • Zero-Width Overlay Scrollbars: Both page body and panel container utilize floating scrollbars without layout shift.
